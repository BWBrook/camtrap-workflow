# Goal 003 - External horizon scan

## Completed-state objective

Produce a decision-ready, current, source-evidenced external horizon scan that determines what Camtrap Workflow should adopt, adapt, build, test in a later spike, defer, reserve for Barry, or reject before any product-facing codebase renovation begins.

The scan must establish whether a genuine post-labelling product gap exists; compare relevant standards, products, workflows, interface and deployment patterns; and translate the evidence into bounded recommendations for interoperability, product architecture, the Judbury demonstration, and software-note positioning. It is not an exhaustive literature review, a competitor feature catalogue, or an implementation goal.

## Why this goal exists

Goal 002 found a mature scientific engine inside a Judbury-shaped, programmer-facing project and recommended renovation rather than rewrite. Several high-leverage decisions cannot responsibly be made from internal evidence alone: the public input posture, provenance concepts, spatial and temporal entities, progressive configuration, no-R delivery, LocalMap interchange, optional regional and AI extensions, and the project's defensible novelty.

Goal 003 resolves those external dependencies before implementation. Research continues only while it changes a defined decision, viable pattern, criterion, or confidence level.

## Established evidence and decisions

### Repo-observed Goal 002 findings

Goal 003 accepts the published Goal 002 audit as its internal baseline:

- current inputs and schemas are too Judbury-specific for progressive capability (`G004`);
- input selection and labelling provenance are absent (`G005`);
- location, deployment interval, and analysis site are collapsed (`G007`);
- all current entrypoints require R or shell knowledge (`G012`);
- the LocalMap_Tas boundary lacks an explicit interchange and provenance contract (`G015`);
- named spatial resolutions and temporal schemes are absent (`G023`);
- regional ALA/gamma context is absent and scientifically bounded (`G024`);
- a safe optional AI interpretation boundary is absent (`G025`).

The audit recommends preserving the scientific engine behind compatibility adapters rather than rewriting it. Goal 003 may refine that recommendation but must not implement it.

### Barry-provided Judbury design fact

The following is Barry-provided study context, not a fact inferred from the current repository:

- the intended design has nine nominal grid stations in a 3 x 3 square;
- four cameras were each relocated once, producing 13 physical locations in total;
- two relocations followed an early service and deliberately placed cameras better on game trails;
- two relocations followed SD-card theft and moved cameras approximately 50 metres from their original positions;
- the study may therefore have more deployment intervals than physical locations.

Goal 003 must preserve `nominal_grid_station`, `physical_location`, `deployment`, and derived `analysis_site` as distinct concepts. It may compare how external standards and products represent those concepts and how named spatial or temporal resolutions are expressed. It may not decide whether any relocated position should be ecologically merged, separated, or grouped for an analysis.

## Required research questions

The scan must answer:

1. What concepts should be adopted from CamtrapDP and other credible standards, and what complexity should remain behind adapters?
2. What is the safest interoperable model for labelled image-level inputs, input-selection scope, labelling provenance, operational records, and row lineage?
3. Which external entity patterns preserve the Judbury hierarchy without pre-deciding ecological grouping?
4. How do credible tools represent progressive input profiles, capability unlocking, module omission, repair policies, and resolved configuration?
5. Which maintained products already turn labelled camera-trap records into ecological evidence, for whom, through what user journey, and with what reproducibility and provenance?
6. Is local/offline, no-R, configuration-driven, audit-rich ecological analysis a real product white space, a narrower integration opportunity, or already well served?
7. Which interface and deployment patterns merit later bounded prototypes, considering installation, update, rollback, operating-system support, privacy, offline use, network dependencies, licensing, and maintainability?
8. Which file, identifier, checksum, manifest, versioning, and error-state patterns should inform the LocalMap_Tas interchange without coupling repositories?
9. What evidence threshold and guardrails would justify later ALA/gamma-diversity or AI-reporting experiments without adding them now?
10. What software-note claim is defensible relative to existing products and papers, with Judbury as a validating demonstration rather than the product itself?

## Research protocol

### Source priority

Use the most current authoritative source available at execution time:

1. normative standards and specifications;
2. official documentation, maintained source repositories, validators, release records, and explicit licence or privacy terms;
3. peer-reviewed papers and software notes;
4. credible independent technical or institutional secondary sources;
5. product or marketing claims, retained only when clearly labelled and not treated as proof of behavior.

Search broadly enough to identify credible candidates, then read primary sources deeply enough to support the decision. Do not infer runtime behavior from a feature claim, repository existence, or paper abstract. Contradictions between specifications, documentation, releases, and papers must remain visible.

### Inclusion

Include evidence that materially informs one or more decision domains and is:

- current or historically necessary to explain an influential pattern;
- attributable to a stable owner and URL;
- specific enough to support a claim, limitation, comparison, or rejection;
- relevant to post-labelling camera-trap analysis, standards/interchange, reproducible local tools, product delivery, or the bounded extensions.

Image detection or classification products may appear only as upstream-boundary evidence or an adjacent comparison. They are not Camtrap Workflow competitors merely because they process camera-trap media.

### Exclusion

Exclude or demote:

- unverified feature lists, generic marketing copy, search-result snippets, unsourced aggregations, and stale mirrors;
- tools whose only relevant function is image detection, classification, collection management, or annotation;
- abandoned or unavailable products unless their historical influence changes a current decision;
- source-code archaeology beyond public documentation and high-level repository metadata;
- evidence requiring an account, login, stakeholder contact, installation, executable download, or runtime trial.

Record material exclusions and their reasons in `Log.md`.

### Candidate and stopping limits

- Compare approximately three to five serious candidates or patterns per decision domain.
- Each major domain must compare at least two credible alternatives or explicitly document an evidence-backed absence of alternatives.
- Extra sources may corroborate a candidate without turning it into another serious candidate.
- One source pass is one bounded search or navigation round within a declared source class for a domain. Initial coverage must include both an authoritative/official pass and a scholarly or credible independent pass.
- Stop domain research when two consecutive follow-up source passes add no decision-changing evidence, viable pattern, criterion, contradiction, or confidence change.
- Do not expand a bounded extension criterion into a broad ALA, gamma-diversity, AI, or journal literature review.

### Decision quality

- CamtrapDP posture, labelled-input provenance, Judbury-compatible entity patterns, progressive profiles, product white space, no-R local delivery, lifecycle/privacy, LocalMap interchange, ALA criteria, AI criteria, and software-note positioning each receive a bounded recommendation.

- Windows, macOS, and Linux are compared without asserting unknown stakeholder preferences or procurement constraints.
- The LocalMap recommendation preserves file-and-manifest coupling, independent versioning, no shared DAG/cache, and no hidden network dependency from Camtrap Workflow.

- The project recommendation is exactly one of `continue`, `narrow`, `integrate`, or `stop` and states what evidence would overturn it.
- The recommendation appears once in the exact form `**Project recommendation:** <value>` so static validation cannot confuse it with prose about alternatives.

- Observations, source claims, inference, recommendations, contradictions, and uncertainty are visibly distinguished.

- Lack of found evidence is reported as `not found under this protocol`, not proof of universal absence.
- No runtime, installation, or product behavior is claimed without direct evidence.
- Decisions outside the executor's authority remain in `decision_register.md` or are explicitly deferred.

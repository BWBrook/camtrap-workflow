# jc_site.csv: columns and descriptions:
site: Unique identifier for each camera site (e.g., jc_cen).
lat: Latitude coordinate (in decimal degrees).
lon: Longitude coordinate (in decimal degrees).
type: Site type classification, indicating accessibility or openness (e.g., open, closed).
location: Site setting or context (e.g., arena, game trail, road).
cover: Ground surface cover at camera site (e.g., dirt, rock).
vegclass: Tasmanian vegetation community classification (e.g., DOB, DTO, FPS).
slope_d: Slope at the camera site, expressed in degrees.
elevation_m: Elevation of site in meters above sea level.
canopy_h_m: Canopy height at site, measured in meters.
fire_flam: Fire flammability rating (e.g., high ('h'), low ('l')).
fire_sens: Sensitivity of vegetation to fire (e.g., low sensitivity ('l')).
